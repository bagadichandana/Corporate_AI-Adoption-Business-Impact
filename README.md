# 🤖 Corporate AI Adoption & Business Impact Dashboard

An interactive **Business Intelligence and AI Analytics project** designed to analyze how organizations are adopting artificial intelligence and how AI investment translates into measurable business outcomes.

The project analyzes **200,000 corporate records** across different industries, countries and years, focusing on AI adoption, investment, automation, productivity, revenue impact, cost savings, employee training and AI maturity.

## 🎯 Project Objective

To understand the relationship between **AI adoption and business performance** and identify patterns in:

* 🤖 AI adoption levels
* 💰 AI investment
* ⚙️ Automation rates
* 📈 Revenue impact
* 💵 Cost savings
* 🚀 Productivity gains
* 👥 Employee AI training
* 📊 AI maturity
* 🏭 Industry-level adoption
* 🌎 Country-level trends
* 📅 Year-wise AI adoption patterns

## 📊 Dataset

**200,000 corporate records**

The dataset contains information on:

`Industry` • `Country` • `Year` • `AI Adoption` • `AI Investment` • `Automation` • `Cost Savings` • `Revenue Impact` • `Productivity Gain` • `Training Hours` • `AI Maturity` • `Deployment Count`

## 🛠️ Skills Demonstrated

**Data Cleaning • Data Transformation • Data Modeling • KPI Development • Data Visualization • Trend Analysis • Business Intelligence • AI Analytics • Business Performance Analysis**

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of corporate AI adoption and helps identify how organizations are progressing from **AI investment and adoption to automation, productivity improvements and financial impact**.
